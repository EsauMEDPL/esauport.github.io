Artist's Statement

Esau Martinez is an Illustration Artist but would love to try out other types of Design Media. Esau does love creating illustrations of pictures of fictional settings, characters, environments, and stories. To tell a story through worldbuilding is something that I always wanted to do. However, I would be lying if other types of Media art did not intrigue me. One of the many inspirations come from, but not limited to, Horror movies such as Barbarian and Backrooms, or series such as, but not limited to, vita carnis and Greylock due to placing the one viewing the piece into a level of discomfort as the story is told from the first person perspective. This allows the art to have more of an impact as the horror is something that the threat is not known or clearly shown. Another source of inspiration are animated shows such as, but not limited to, Adventure Time, X-Men 97’, or Frieren Beyond Journey End. Many and many shows come from fictional narratives which allow for creativity to show and sometimes mirror the world around us. The environment is an amazing storytelling tool but also a showcase of the aesthetic a show has. My aesthetics that commonly appear in my artwork are horror and cartoony. My Art is a representation of myself and my ideas that come to mind. What I hope to achieve with my art is to invoke a feeling, whether strong or faint.
